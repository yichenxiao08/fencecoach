# Confidence and capture quality

Pose confidence below 0.65 is an engineering threshold for this prototype, not a validated biomechanical standard. Flag low-confidence measurements for manual review and avoid using them to justify a technique correction.

Masks, weapons, loose clothing, occluded joints, motion blur, and another person in frame can interfere with landmark estimation. Before re-recording, use a fixed side-on camera with the whole body and relevant footwork area visible. Review which joints or annotated event times were uncertain.

A numeric confidence value is supplied by the measurement producer; the coaching agent cannot independently verify it. Neither confidence nor a valid citation ID proves that a claim is accurate. Human review of evidence and conclusions remains necessary.
