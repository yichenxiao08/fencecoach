# Footwork rhythm review

For advance and retreat practice, review repeatability within a fixed camera setup. A sequence of step intervals can describe rhythm but does not measure tactical effectiveness or readiness against an opponent.

Practice prompt: alternate one advance and one retreat, pause in en garde, and repeat for three short sets. Use a qualified coach's review to decide whether balance and starting position are restored. Do not optimize speed at the expense of control.

Apparent stance width in an uncalibrated side-view clip is a screen-space measurement. Frame width or pixels are not centimeters. Changing camera distance, angle, cropping, or perspective changes apparent distances, so compare only recordings with consistent capture conditions.
