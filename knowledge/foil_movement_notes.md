# Foil movement notes (starter knowledge)

These notes are a small, self-authored retrieval corpus for the prototype. They are practice prompts, not universal coaching rules. Adapt them with feedback from a qualified fencing coach.

## Lunge and recovery

A useful lunge review separates preparation, extension, front-foot landing, and recovery. Compare repetitions from the same camera setup. Look for a controlled return to en garde and repeatable balance rather than maximizing one isolated distance measurement.

Suggested drill: perform five slow lunges with a deliberate recovery to en garde, then five at normal practice speed. Rest between sets. Review the side-view clips and note which repetitions stay balanced.

## Advance and retreat

For a short footwork review, compare step rhythm and the ability to return to a stable en garde position. Avoid comparing pixel distances from clips recorded at different camera positions unless the footage has been calibrated.

Suggested drill: alternate one advance and one retreat for three short sets. Keep the camera fixed and review whether the starting guard position is restored after each sequence.

## Measurement limitations

Two-dimensional pose landmarks can be noisy when a weapon, mask, loose clothing, or another person occludes joints. Camera perspective affects apparent distances. Treat low-confidence landmarks and uncalibrated pixel distances as uncertain evidence, not as precise biomechanics.
