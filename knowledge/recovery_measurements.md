# Reading recovery measurements

A recovery-time measurement describes the elapsed interval between two annotated events. It does not establish balance, correctness, tactical quality, or whether a fencer is ready for the next action. Review the event timestamps and the same-camera clip before drawing a technique conclusion.

For baseline comparisons, use recordings made with the same camera setup and the same event definitions. A negative recovery-time delta means the measured interval decreased. A positive delta means it increased. Neither direction alone proves an improvement. Never compare a duration in milliseconds to one in seconds without explicit conversion.

If repetitions have different confidence, display the confidence and sample count beside any mean. Low-confidence repetitions should be flagged for manual review; do not silently remove them and present the remaining mean as the whole session.
